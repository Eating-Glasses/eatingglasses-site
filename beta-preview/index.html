Help test a new diabetic app that works with your current blood sugar readings, identifies hidden patterns, makes personalized suggestions, and guides you on the results.

What it does

Eating Glasses reads the glucose data from your CGM, finds the patterns in it (the highs, lows and steady stretches that keep coming back) and explains them in plain language. No manual logging.

How it helps

It suggests small, safe, everyday experiments chosen for your data, then checks whether your numbers actually moved. Detect, try, track, results: over time you learn what works for your body, not someone else's average. Your glucose stays on your phone, and the app never tells you anything about insulin or doses.

Why your feedback matters

This app is built for diabetics by diabetics. The more you let us know about how the app is and isn't working for you, the better we can make it for all of us. While we all have unique journeys, we share many of the same problems and solutions. Thanks for being part of the solution.
