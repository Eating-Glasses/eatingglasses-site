What it does

Eating Glasses reads the glucose data from your CGM, finds the patterns in it (the highs, lows and steady stretches that keep coming back) and explains them in plain language. No manual logging.

How it helps

It suggests small, everyday steps chosen for your data, then checks whether your numbers actually moved. Detect, try, track, results: over time you see what changed in your own numbers, not someone else's average. Your CGM readings never leave your phone, and Eating Glasses never suggests an insulin dose.

Why your feedback matters

This app is built for people with diabetes, by people with diabetes. The more you share about how the app is and isn't working for you, the better it gets for everyone who uses it. Every journey is different, but many of the problems and solutions are shared. Thanks for being part of the solution.
