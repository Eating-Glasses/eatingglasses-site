Before you start

If your CGM shares to Apple Health (Dexcom, Stelo or Eversense), turn that on in its app before you open Eating Glasses.

Dexcom G7

First, in the Dexcom G7 app

  1. Open the Dexcom G7 app.
  2. Tap the Connections tab.
  3. Tap Apple Health.
  4. Tap "Activate to share."
  5. Turn on Blood Glucose.
  6. Tap Allow.

Then, in Eating Glasses

Open Eating Glasses and follow the screens. When Apple Health asks, turn on Blood Glucose, then tap Allow.

Your newest readings in Eating Glasses will be about 3 hours behind your Dexcom app. That is normal.

Dexcom G6

First, in the Dexcom G6 app

  1. Open the Dexcom G6 app.
  2. Open the menu.
  3. Tap Settings.
  4. Tap Health.
  5. Tap Enable.
  6. Turn on Blood Glucose.
  7. Tap Done.

Menu names can differ a little between versions of the Dexcom app.

Then, in Eating Glasses

Open Eating Glasses and follow the screens. When Apple Health asks, turn on Blood Glucose, then tap Allow.

Your newest readings in Eating Glasses will be about 3 hours behind your Dexcom app. That is normal.

Stelo

First, in the Stelo app

  1. Open the Stelo app.
  2. Tap Settings.
  3. Tap Connections.
  4. Follow the steps on screen for Apple Health.
  5. If Apple Health asks, turn on Blood Glucose and tap Allow.

Then, in Eating Glasses

Open Eating Glasses and follow the screens. When Apple Health asks, turn on Blood Glucose, then tap Allow.

Your newest readings in Eating Glasses will be about 3 hours behind your Stelo app. That is normal.

Eversense

First, in the Eversense app

  1. Open the Eversense app.
  2. Tap Menu.
  3. Tap "Share My Data."
  4. Tap "Application Sharing."
  5. Tap Apple Health.
  6. Turn on "Share Data."
  7. If Apple Health asks, turn on Blood Glucose and tap Allow.

Menu names can differ a little between versions of the Eversense app.

Then, in Eating Glasses

Open Eating Glasses and follow the screens. When Apple Health asks, turn on Blood Glucose, then tap Allow.

FreeStyle Libre 2 or 3 (LibreView)

Libre doesn't share readings with Apple Health, so you bring your history in from a LibreView file instead.

Get your file

  1. Use a computer. It is the simplest way.
  2. Open libreview.com. If it asks, pick United States and English.
  3. Sign in with the same email and password as your Libre app.
  4. Click the Glucose History icon, then Download Glucose Data.
  5. Tick I'm not a robot, then click Download.
  6. Send the file to your iPhone with AirDrop, or email it to yourself and save it to Files. Do not open or re-save it in Excel.

Bring it into Eating Glasses

  1. On the connect screen, tap "My CGM isn't in Apple Health" (the link under the big button).
  2. Choose Freestyle Libre 3 or Freestyle Libre 2, then tap "Import my LibreView data".
  3. Tap Choose file and pick your CSV.

Already past setup? Tap Settings, then Import new data, then Choose file. To refresh your data later, export a new file and import it the same way.

Medtronic (MiniMed, Guardian)

Medtronic doesn't share readings with Apple Health, so you bring your history in from a CareLink file instead.

Get your file (easiest on a computer)

  1. Open carelink.minimed.com and sign in.
  2. Go to Reports.
  3. Choose Data export (CSV).
  4. Pick the widest date range.
  5. Save the CSV, then get it onto your iPhone: email it to yourself and save the attachment to Files, or put it in iCloud Drive.

Bring it into Eating Glasses

  1. On the connect screen, tap "My CGM isn't in Apple Health" (the link under the big button).
  2. Choose Medtronic (MiniMed, Guardian), then tap "Import my CareLink data".
  3. Tap Choose file and pick your CSV.

Already past setup? Tap Settings, then Import new data, then Choose file. If that screen shows Dexcom instructions, pick your CareLink file anyway: it reads CareLink files too. To refresh your data later, export a new file and import it the same way.

No readings came through?

Do these in order. Tap Try again after each one.

  1. In the Health app, tap your picture or initials, then Apps, then Eating Glasses, and turn on Blood Glucose.
  2. In that same list, open your CGM app (for example Dexcom G7) and turn on Blood Glucose there too.
  3. In your CGM app, check that Apple Health sharing is on. If it is already on, turn it off and back on.
  4. Still stuck? Email support@eatingglasses.com with your CGM and what the screen says.

Or bring a file in (Dexcom Clarity, LibreView or CareLink): tap Import my history on that screen.

Stuck, or something looks off?

Take a screenshot inside the app. TestFlight will offer Share Beta Feedback, which sends the screenshot straight to Eating Glasses with a note from you. You can also email support@eatingglasses.com.

Your CGM readings never leave your phone, and Eating Glasses never suggests an insulin dose.
